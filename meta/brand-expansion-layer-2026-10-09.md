## 7. Creative-fatigue rotation (design, no automatic writes)

Flag an ad when, over the last 7 days, its frequency > 2.5, or its ThruPlay rate drops > 20 % below its
first-week level, or its cost per ThruPlay rises > 30 %. Action (prompted): build the next bench creative
from §2 as a new ad in BR-1, read back, then pause the flagged ad — never delete it. Review cadence: the
daily routine flags, the Monday 09:22 run proposes the swap. Rotate at most two ads per week so the ad set
keeps a stable learning signal. Refresh the bench with new footage every 4–6 weeks (ask the team for
process reels: steam on grease, extractor filter, inside cabinets, before/after walkthrough, team
arriving with equipment, customer reaction).
