We provide deep cleaning only (kitchen deep cleaning and full home deep cleaning). You book an arrival window (8:00–12:00, 12:00–16:00 or 16:00–21:00). The team arrives within the window; a delay of up to one hour is possible because of traffic, and we will keep you informed on WhatsApp.

3. Your side

Please make sure that running water and electricity are available and that someone can open the door at the start of the window. Valuables, cash, documents and fragile items should be put away before the team arrives.

4. Payment

Payment is made on completion of the work: cash, card payment link or bank transfer. No deposit is required for standard bookings.

5. Reschedule

One free reschedule if requested more than 24 hours before the booking, subject to availability. Less than 24 hours before: a reschedule is possible only against a deposit that is credited to the new booking. A second reschedule counts as a cancellation.

6. Cancellation

More than 12 hours before the booking: no charge. 12 to 8 hours before: 30% of the booking price. Less than 8 hours before and before the team is dispatched: 50%. After dispatch, on arrival, or if the team cannot access the property: 100%. If we have to cancel, there is no charge and we offer you the earliest available slot.

7. Quality guarantee

Report any issue to the team on site or to us within 24 hours and we redo that part free of charge. We do not repair pre-existing damage, stains that cannot be removed without damaging the surface, or wear and tear.
