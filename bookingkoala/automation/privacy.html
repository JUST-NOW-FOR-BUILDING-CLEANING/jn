Privacy Policy – Just Now For Building Cleaning Services LLC

Just Now For Building Cleaning Services LLC ("Just Now", "we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect when you book with us and how we use it.

1. What we collect

When you book we collect your name, phone number, email address, the service address, the service you chose, your preferred date and arrival window and any notes you add for the team. If you contact us on WhatsApp we also keep that conversation.

2. Why we use it

We use your details only to confirm, schedule, perform and follow up your cleaning booking, to send you booking confirmations and reminders, to answer your questions and to handle payment and any complaint or redo request.

3. Who sees it

Your name, address, phone number and booking notes are shared with the cleaning team assigned to your job. Our booking system is provided by BookingKoala, which stores the data on our behalf. We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.

4. Marketing

We send marketing messages only if you agreed to receive them. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP on WhatsApp or emailing us.

5. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep booking records for as long as needed for accounting and service history. You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete your personal data by emailing justnow.cs@gmail.com.

6. Cookies

This booking page uses cookies that are needed for the page to work and basic analytics. You can block cookies in your browser; the booking form will still work.
